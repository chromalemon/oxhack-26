Playmaker.

What does the app do?
Playmaker is a webapp that allows the user to place points onto a football pitch simply by clicking, and they will be provided with a list of potential next steps / plays, and the probability of success for each one. The platform will work by training a model on historical data, to understand which plays lead to what outcomes. There are many things to take into account when analysing scenarios such as team level, mentality (perhaps), current score, the various plays possible, the depth of each play, the success criteria of a play, and the success rate of each option.

What is the minimum viable product?
The MVP is a webapp which the user can interact with through a UI, configuring options and placing player and ball locations. The user clicks a button, and after some processing the webapp displays the potential plays that could be made, and makes a prediction of which one is most likely to "succeed".

What are the design rules?
The design rules are not super strict. I am imagining a sleek but basic white interfact which a simplistic 2d football pitch which the user places points onto, and the predictions are explained as test or perhaps animations.
