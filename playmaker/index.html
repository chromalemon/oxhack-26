Guide

How Playmaker works

1. Set the scene Place or drag up to 11 players for either side and the ball. Apply a formation to fill a team shape.

2. Shape the context Score, possession, cards, substitutions, mentality, and risk all change the recommendation.

3. Compare options Analyse the scene. Safest, highest upside, and best-balance picks are listed above the ranked plays.

4. Watch the exact pitch Open a play to replay every placed player, opponent pressure, and passing lane on a timeline.

5. Keep the work Undo, duplicate, annotate, export, or share. Drafts autosave in this browser.
