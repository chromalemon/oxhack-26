Guide

How Playmaker works

1. Set the scene Choose a team tool, then place up to 11 players for either side and the ball on the pitch.

2. Shape the context Adjust game state, mentality, and risk appetite to describe the decision.

3. Compare options Analyse the scene, then select a recommendation to see its movement and tactical explanation.

4. Edit quickly Choose Brush and drag around the pitch to erase every player inside the brush ring.

5. Keep the work Save scenarios to your account or continue locally when the API is unavailable.
